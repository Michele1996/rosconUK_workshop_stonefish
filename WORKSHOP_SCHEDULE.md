# Workshop Schedule — 3 hours

## 00:00–00:20 — Welcome to marine robotics

Why marine robots are different: buoyancy, drag, currents, limited
communication, GPS availability at the surface, underwater sensing and optical
degradation.

## 00:20–00:45 — Stonefish

Architecture, scenario files, geometry-based simulation, sensors, actuators,
ROS 2 interfaces and the difference between an ideal actuator and a dynamic
thruster model.

## 00:45–01:15 — Exercise 1: BlueBoat

Inspect the USV, ROS topics and two-thruster differential actuation. Run a
simple path-following task.

## 01:15–01:25 — Short break

## 01:25–01:50 — Exercise 2: BlueROV2

Move from planar surface navigation to a multi-thruster underwater vehicle.
Inspect the camera, DVL, pressure/depth, IMU and odometry interfaces.

## 01:50–02:30 — Challenge: survey the site

Plan and execute a survey that produces sufficient image overlap for
reconstruction.

## 02:30–02:55 — From images to 3D

Export the acquired images and run the provided COLMAP/GLOMAP reconstruction
workflow (or use the fallback dataset).

## 02:55–03:00 — Results and wrap-up

Compare survey strategies and reconstructions; discuss how simulation can be
used for autonomy, perception and machine-learning research.
