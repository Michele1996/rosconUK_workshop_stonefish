# Equipment and Room Requirements

For the organisers:

- projector/screen with HDMI;
- reliable power at participant tables;
- Wi-Fi/internet access;
- presenter table with power;
- space for participants to use laptops;
- ideally one instructor machine with a dedicated GPU and the full workshop
  environment pre-installed.

Participants bring their own laptops.

No physical marine robot, tank or wet equipment is required.
